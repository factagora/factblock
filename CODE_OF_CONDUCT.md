# Code of conduct

This project is a place to work on a format and a library. Everyone is welcome to report, ask, propose and
contribute, whoever they are and wherever they come from.

**Expected**: be direct and be kind. Disagree with the idea, not the person. Assume the other side has a reason
you have not heard yet. Say what you need; answer what was asked.

**Not accepted**: harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory remarks, publishing someone's private information,
and sustained disruption of a thread after being asked to stop.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads and, for repeated or severe cases, block an account from
the repository. Report a problem to randy@factagora.com; reports are read by a person and handled in private.
